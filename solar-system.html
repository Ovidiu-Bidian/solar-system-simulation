How to use the orrery

Pick any body and it becomes the still centre of the view. The glowing trails are the paths every other body traces relative to it — from Earth, Mars draws its retrograde loops and Venus a five-petalled rose (lengthen trails with ] to see all 8 years of it).

Positions use JPL's approximate Keplerian elements (most accurate 1800–2050), so the sky you see for today's date is the real one. Distances are true to scale unless Compact is on; planet sizes and the Moon's distance from Earth are exaggerated so you can see them. The grid lies in the ecliptic plane through the centre body.
